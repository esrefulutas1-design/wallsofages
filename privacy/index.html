Walls of Ages – Privacy Policy Last updated: October 31, 2026 This privacy policy explains how Three Stones ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Walls of Ages (the "Game"). 1. Information we collect We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (levels, gold, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data. 2. Advertising The Game is free and shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob and, through AdMob mediation, by Unity Ads and AppLovin. These partners may automatically collect and process: the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), IP address and approximate (country/city level) location derived from it, device information (model, operating system, language, screen size), ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, video completions) and diagnostic data. This data is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, show personalized ads. Each partner processes data under its own privacy policy: Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses data from partner apps Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ 3. Your choices Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the Game's consent screen. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy › Tracking). The optional "No Ads" purchase removes banner and interstitial ads. 4. In-app purchases Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. 5. Children The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it. 6. Data sharing and security We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and store partners listed above, we do not share data with third parties. Data sent by the partners' SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). 7. Changes We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. 8.
